# Evaluation protocol revision after EXP0062 access

The 2700000000 test range was accidentally used by EXP0062 with offset 11.
Those 14 scene seeds and all N/condition variants are development-only and
remain archived. They are excluded from any final claim. No replacement scene
was selected based on outcome. The access ledger records the source commit,
purpose and complete anatomy list. Remaining test offsets stay provisionally
sealed until the method is frozen and a historical access audit is complete.

The multi-cluster seed guard now covers both per-anatomy test ranges and the
registered multi-cluster benchmark range. A future sealed run must fail closed
on any recorded development seed.
