Fig. 2b | On-policy learning of the shared semantic-candidate actor. Top, trailing mean of navigation success over 15 consecutive training episodes against cumulative agent steps for three independent IPPO seeds on the nine-anatomy pool; the band spans the seed minimum to maximum. Bottom, the curriculum level actually sampled in each episode. Success is non-monotonic by construction: the curriculum promotes difficulty as the policy masters the easier levels, so the mid-run dip coincides with the easy-to-hard transition rather than with forgetting. Whole-run training success was 59.7%, 55.7%, 54.7% for the three seeds. Curves are training-time episodes under exploration, not the deterministic evaluation protocol.
