Fig. 4c,d | Robustness and safety cost across flow and obstacle conditions on the HCC territory. Each point is the mean over 12 paired scene seeds; shaded band (ours) and error bars (baselines) are bootstrap 95% intervals over those seeds (10⁴ resamples). Every method receives the identical global layer, safety layer and scene seeds, so only the local controller differs. c, Safe success. d, Wall contact per episode. Conditions increase inlet flow and dynamic-obstacle count together, from 0.025 mm/s with 4 obstacles to 0.15 mm/s with 32. The switchable-TPG baseline is bit-identical to pursuit on this territory because the construct yields no switchable conflict zone (see Methods). Intervals are shown for the learned policy and for the strongest classical rule; the remaining baselines are drawn as means only for legibility. The wide interval on the learned policy reflects its low success rate at n=12 paired seeds, not extra variance in the scenes.
