Fig. 3c | Representative navigation outcomes. Top, cluster trajectories in the x-z projection: squares mark entry stations, stars mark arrival, crosses mark the final pose of an episode that timed out, and orange circles are the registered target sites. Bottom, the distance from each cluster to its assigned target over time, which is what separates the three outcomes: direct arrival (15 s, 3/3 reached), delayed arrival (169 s, 3/3 reached), timeout (300 s, 2/3 reached). A direct arrival descends monotonically through the 0.30 mm reach tolerance; a delayed arrival plateaus or rises before descending; a timeout stalls at a finite distance for the remainder of the 300 s horizon. The three episodes are the fastest success, the slowest success and the furthest-progressing failure among recorded rollouts on frozen manifest rows, so they bound the observed spread rather than being selected by appearance.
